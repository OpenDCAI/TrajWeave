## Upstream work

The retained training backend is derived from [VERL](https://github.com/volcengine/verl).
The integrated methods build on the work of their original research teams.
Original copyright headers, [Notice.txt](Notice.txt), and the files under
[licenses/](licenses/) preserve third-party attribution and terms.

## Public history

The public Git history begins with a consolidated code snapshot. Its initial
commit credits the authors whose contributions are included in that snapshot.
Earlier commit counts and line-by-line blame are not reproduced by that commit;
the acknowledgements above preserve the distinction between current and earlier work.
Future contributions are recorded through normal commits and pull requests.
